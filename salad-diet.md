# Daily breakfast recipe

## Yoghurt, kefir, oats, berries + egg and peanut butter toast

**Have this every day.**

### Ingredients

| Ingredient                                |               Amount |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------: |
| Plain Greek yoghurt                       |        **150–200 g** |
| Kefir                                     |           **200 ml** |
| Oats                                      |          **50–60 g** |
| Raspberries, blackberries or strawberries |        **100–150 g** |
| Wholemeal or seeded bread                 |          **1 slice** |
| Peanut butter                             | **1 tbsp / 15–20 g** |
| Egg                                       |                **1** |

### Preparation

1. Add yoghurt, kefir and oats to a bowl.
2. Stir well.
3. Top with berries.
4. Toast the bread and spread with peanut butter.
5. Boil, poach or scramble 1 egg.
6. Eat together as breakfast.

**Meal prep option:** mix yoghurt, kefir and oats the night before to make overnight oats. Add berries in the morning.

# Day 1

## 1. Chicken Caesar Crunch Salad

**Protein:** chicken portion 1

### Ingredients

| Ingredient                             |        Amount |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------: |
| Romaine lettuce                        |      **80 g** |
| Spinach                                |      **50 g** |
| Cooked chicken breast                  |     **120 g** |
| Cherry tomatoes                        |     **100 g** |
| Parmesan                               |   **10–15 g** |
| Wholegrain croutons                    |   **20–25 g** |
| Caesar dressing, preferably lower-salt | **1–1½ tbsp** |
| Lemon juice                            |     **1 tsp** |
| Black pepper                           |      to taste |

### Preparation

1. Chop romaine and spinach.
2. Slice the cooked chicken.
3. Halve the cherry tomatoes.
4. Add romaine, spinach, tomatoes and chicken to a bowl.
5. Add parmesan and croutons.
6. Drizzle with Caesar dressing just before eating.
7. Finish with lemon juice and black pepper.

**Prep tip:** keep croutons and dressing separate until serving.

## 2. Greek Chickpea Rainbow Salad

### Ingredients

| Ingredient                    |               Amount |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------: |
| Cucumber                      |            **100 g** |
| Tomatoes                      |            **120 g** |
| Red onion                     |             **30 g** |
| Bell pepper                   |             **80 g** |

| Olive oil                     |            **2 tsp** |
| Dried oregano                 |            **½ tsp** |

# Day 2

## 3. Tuna Niçoise-style Salad, no egg

**Fish portion:** tuna

### Ingredients

| Ingredient             |                      Amount |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------: |
| Rocket/arugula         |                    **60 g** |
| Tuna, drained          | **1 small can / 100–120 g** |
| Baby potatoes          |                   **150 g** |
| Green beans            |                    **80 g** |
| Tomatoes               |                   **100 g** |
| Olives                 |                    **20 g** |
| Olive oil              |                   **2 tsp** |
| Lemon juice or vinegar |                  **1 tbsp** |
| Dijon mustard          |                   **1 tsp** |
| Black pepper           |                    to taste |

### Preparation

1. Boil baby potatoes for 12–15 minutes until tender. Cool and halve.
2. Boil or steam green beans for 3–4 minutes, then cool.
3. Add rocket to a bowl.
4. Add potatoes, green beans, tuna, tomatoes and olives.
5. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, mustard and black pepper.
6. Drizzle over the salad before eating.

**Note:** no egg here because you already have 1 egg at breakfast.

## 4. Salmon Avocado Citrus Salad

**Fish portion:** salmon
**Oily fish portion:** yes

### Ingredients

| Ingredient          |               Amount |
| ------------------- | -------------------: |
| Spinach             |             **70 g** |
| Salmon fillet       |        **150 g raw** |
| Avocado             |  **½ medium / 70 g** |
| Cucumber            |             **80 g** |
| Radishes            |             **40 g** |
| Lemon or lime juice |           **1 tbsp** |
| Olive oil           |            **2 tsp** |
| Black pepper        |             to taste |
| Iodised salt        | tiny pinch, optional |

### Preparation

1. Season salmon with black pepper and a tiny pinch of iodised salt if using.
2. Cook salmon:

   * Pan-fry: 3–4 minutes each side, or
   * Bake: 180°C for 12–15 minutes.
3. Let salmon cool slightly, then flake into large pieces.
4. Slice avocado, cucumber and radishes.
5. Add spinach to a bowl.
6. Top with salmon, avocado, cucumber and radishes.
7. Mix lemon or lime juice with olive oil.
8. Drizzle over the salad.

**Prep tip:** add avocado fresh if possible.

## 6. Quinoa Roasted Veg Salad

### Ingredients

| Ingredient                |      Amount |
| ------------------------- | ----------: |
| Quinoa, dry               |    **45 g** |
| Spinach                   |    **60 g** |
| Sweet potato              |   **120 g** |
| Courgette/zucchini        |   **100 g** |
| Mushrooms                 |    **80 g** |
| Bell pepper               |    **80 g** |
| Feta                      | **25–30 g** |
| Olive oil                 |   **2 tsp** |
| Lemon juice               |  **1 tbsp** |
| Smoked paprika or oregano |   **½ tsp** |
| Black pepper              |    to taste |

### Preparation

1. Rinse quinoa and cook according to packet instructions, usually 12–15 minutes.
2. Dice sweet potato, courgette, mushrooms and bell pepper.
3. Toss vegetables with 1 tsp olive oil, paprika or oregano and black pepper.
4. Roast at 200°C for 20–25 minutes.
5. Add spinach to a bowl.
6. Add cooked quinoa and roasted vegetables.
7. Crumble feta over the top.
8. Mix lemon juice with the remaining 1 tsp olive oil.
9. Drizzle over the salad.

**Prep tip:** this is good warm or cold.

## 9. Tofu Peanut Crunch Salad

### Ingredients

| Ingredient               |      Amount |
| ------------------------ | ----------: |
| Firm tofu                |   **150 g** |
| Red cabbage              |    **80 g** |
| Carrot                   |    **60 g** |
| Cucumber                 |    **80 g** |
| Edamame                  |    **75 g** |
| Spring onion             |       **1** |
| Peanuts                  | **10–15 g** |
| Fresh coriander/cilantro |  **1 tbsp** |

### Peanut-lime dressing

| Ingredient         |              Amount |
| ------------------ | ------------------: |
| Peanut butter      |          **1 tbsp** |
| Lime juice         |          **1 tbsp** |
| Low-salt soy sauce |           **1 tsp** |
| Warm water         |        **1–2 tbsp** |
| Garlic             | **½ clove, grated** |
| Chilli flakes      |            optional |

### Preparation

1. Press tofu with kitchen paper to remove excess water.
2. Dice tofu into cubes.
3. Pan-fry tofu in a non-stick pan for 6–8 minutes until golden.
4. Cook edamame if frozen, then cool.
5. Slice cabbage, carrot, cucumber and spring onion.
6. Mix peanut butter, lime juice, low-salt soy sauce, garlic and warm water until smooth.
7. Add tofu, vegetables and edamame to a bowl.
8. Drizzle with dressing.
9. Top with peanuts and coriander.

**Prep tip:** keep dressing separate so the vegetables stay crunchy.

| Lime juice                      |            **1 tbsp** |

## 14. Couscous Roasted Courgette Chickpea Mint Salad

### Ingredients

| Ingredient                    |      Amount |
| ----------------------------- | ----------: |
| Couscous, dry                 | **55–60 g** |
| Chickpeas, drained and rinsed |   **120 g** |
| Courgette/zucchini            |   **120 g** |
| Bell pepper                   |   **100 g** |
| Rocket/arugula                |    **50 g** |
| Fresh mint                    |  **1 tbsp** |
| Lemon juice                   |  **1 tbsp** |
| Olive oil                     |   **2 tsp** |
| Black pepper                  |    to taste |
| Chilli flakes                 |    optional |

### Preparation

1. Put couscous in a bowl.
2. Cover with boiling water according to packet instructions.
3. Cover for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
4. Dice courgette and bell pepper.
5. Toss vegetables with 1 tsp olive oil and black pepper.
6. Roast at 200°C for 18–22 minutes.
7. Add rocket to a bowl.
8. Add couscous, roasted vegetables and chickpeas.
9. Mix lemon juice with the remaining 1 tsp olive oil.
10. Drizzle over the salad.
11. Finish with chopped mint and optional chilli flakes.

**Adjustment:** this replaces the old halloumi version.

# Prep notes

* Cook chicken for both chicken salads at once: about **250 g cooked chicken total**.
* Cook quinoa, pasta, couscous and potatoes ahead.
* Roast sweet potato, courgette, mushrooms and peppers together.
* Keep dressings separate.
* Keep avocado, pear and berries as fresh as possible.
* Use iodised salt only lightly.
* Use low-salt soy sauce and lower-salt Caesar dressing.
