## 🥣 **Breakfast Recipes**

### 1. **Oatmeal with Berries & Chia Seeds**

**Ingredients:**

* ½ cup rolled oats
* 1 cup unsweetened almond milk (or water)
* ½ cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
* 1 tsp chia seeds
* Optional: cinnamon or a drizzle of honey

**Instructions:**

1. Bring milk to a simmer, then stir in oats.
2. Cook 5–7 minutes until soft.
3. Top with berries, chia seeds, and cinnamon.

✅ *Rich in fiber and antioxidants to help reduce liver fat.*

---

### 2. **Green Smoothie**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 cup spinach or kale
* ½ banana
* ½ cup pineapple or green apple
* 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
* 1 cup water or unsweetened almond milk

**Instructions:**

1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.
2. Drink immediately.

✅ *Packed with chlorophyll and fiber for liver detox and digestion.*

## 🥗 **Lunch Recipes**

### 3. **Salmon & Quinoa Bowl**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 salmon fillet (about 4 oz)
* ½ cup cooked quinoa
* ½ cup steamed broccoli
* ½ avocado (sliced)
* 1 tbsp olive oil and lemon juice for dressing

**Instructions:**

1. Grill or bake salmon for 12–15 minutes at 375°F (190°C).
2. Serve over quinoa with broccoli and avocado.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon.

✅ *High in omega-3s and antioxidants.*

---

### 4. **Lentil & Veggie Soup**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 cup lentils (rinsed)
* 1 chopped carrot
* 1 diced onion
* 2 cloves garlic
* 1 tsp turmeric
* 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth

**Instructions:**

1. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil for 2 minutes.
2. Add carrots, turmeric, and lentils.
3. Pour in broth and simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are soft.

✅ *Anti-inflammatory, high in plant protein, and low in fat.*

## 🍽️ **Dinner Recipes**

### 5. **Grilled Chicken with Steamed Greens**

**Ingredients:**

* 1 skinless chicken breast
* 1 cup steamed spinach or kale
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* Lemon, pepper, and herbs for flavor

**Instructions:**

1. Season chicken with herbs and grill or bake for 15–20 minutes.
2. Steam greens until tender and drizzle with olive oil and lemon.

✅ *Lean protein supports liver regeneration.*

---

### 6. **Vegetable Stir-Fry with Tofu**

**Ingredients:**

* ½ block firm tofu (cubed)
* 1 cup mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, carrots)
* 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
* 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or coconut aminos
* 1 clove garlic, minced

**Instructions:**

1. Sauté garlic and tofu until golden.
2. Add vegetables and stir-fry 5–7 minutes.
3. Add soy sauce and toss well.

✅ *Low in saturated fat and rich in antioxidants.*
