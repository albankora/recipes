### 🥦 **Foods That Are Good for Fatty Liver**

#### **1. Vegetables and Fruits**

* **Cruciferous vegetables** (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts) – help detoxify the liver and reduce fat accumulation.
* **Leafy greens** (spinach, kale, arugula) – high in antioxidants and chlorophyll, which support liver function.
* **Citrus fruits** (lemons, oranges, grapefruits) – vitamin C and naringenin may help reduce inflammation and fat storage.
* **Berries** (blueberries, raspberries) – rich in polyphenols that protect the liver from oxidative stress.

---

#### **2. Whole Grains**

* **Oats, brown rice, quinoa, barley** – high in fiber, which helps regulate blood sugar and reduce fat buildup in the liver.
* **Avoid refined grains** like white bread or pastries.

---

#### **3. Lean Proteins**

* **Fish (salmon, sardines, tuna, mackerel)** – omega-3 fatty acids reduce liver inflammation and fat levels.
* **Skinless poultry**, **tofu**, **legumes**, and **beans** – provide protein without excess saturated fat.

---

#### **4. Healthy Fats**

* **Olive oil**, **avocado**, **nuts**, and **seeds** – monounsaturated fats improve cholesterol and reduce liver fat.
* **Omega-3 supplements** (if approved by a doctor) may also help reduce liver fat.

---

#### **5. Coffee and Green Tea**

* **Coffee** (without sugar or cream) – studies show it may protect against liver fibrosis and inflammation.
* **Green tea** – rich in catechins, which improve liver enzyme levels and fat metabolism.

---

### 🚫 **Foods to Avoid**

* **Sugary foods and drinks** – soda, candy, pastries, and fruit juices worsen fat buildup.
* **Alcohol** – even small amounts can worsen liver damage.
* **Fried and processed foods** – high in trans and saturated fats that stress the liver.
* **Red and processed meats** – can contribute to inflammation.
* **Refined carbs** – white bread, pasta, and rice can spike blood sugar.

---

### 💧 **Lifestyle Tips**

* Maintain a **healthy weight** and engage in **regular exercise** (at least 150 minutes/week).
* Stay **hydrated** and avoid sugary beverages.
* Manage **blood sugar and cholesterol** levels.
