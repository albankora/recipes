## 12. Fermented foods for gut health

| Item                                   |                   Buy approx. |        Portion guide | Notes                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------: | -------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sauerkraut, raw/live                   |               **1 small jar** |         **1–2 tbsp** | Choose refrigerated if possible; avoid sweetened versions.                       |
| Kimchi                                 |               **1 small jar** |         **1–2 tbsp** | Good with eggs, chicken, tofu or salads. Watch salt.                             |
| Miso paste                             |               **1 small tub** |            **1 tsp** | Use in dressing or soup; do not boil hard if you want to preserve live cultures. |
| Tempeh                                 |           **200–250 g block** |        **100–125 g** | Fermented soy protein; can replace tofu some weeks.                              |
| Fermented pickles / cornichons         |                     **1 jar** | **1–2 small pieces** | Choose salt-brined, no added sugar; not just vinegar-pickled.                    |
| Natto                                  |    **optional, 1 small pack** |    **small portion** | Very nutritious fermented soy, but strong taste/texture.                         |
